Eat

Farm to fork plates, fast food favorites, sushi and real fruit ice lollies.

  • Kitchen & catering

    Food & Stuff

    Fresh, farm to fork cooking from one of Rwanda's leading caterers, with sandwiches, pitas and chapatis made to order. The kitchen doubles as a training ground for local hospitality talent.

    Daily · 11 am to 9 pm@foodandstuffrw

  • Ice lollies

    Laza

    Handmade ice lollies from real fruit grown by Rwandan farmers, naturally sweetened and dairy free. Real fruit, real flavor, made in Rwanda.

    Daily · 11 am to 9 pm@lazainafrica

  • Fast food

    Mr Chips

    A Kigali fast food favorite since 2011, serving crispy fries, burgers, wings and gyros with a taste of Canada. Great food, fast.

    Daily · 11 am to 9:30 pm@mrchipskigaliofficial

    Wok Spot

Shop

Jewelry, fashion, sportswear and design from Rwanda and across Africa.

  • African fashion & design

    AfroQuality

    Fashion, footwear and children's books from dozens of African brands across nine countries and counting, Rwandan designers included. Think of it as a curated passport to the continent.

    Daily · 10 am to 8 pm@afroquality

  • Rwandan art & design

    Art Rwanda

    The shop of Art Rwanda Ubuhanzi, the national program that discovers and supports young Rwandan creatives. Browse fashion, art and design made by the talent it backs.

    Daily · 10 am to 7 pm@ubuhanzi

  • Sportswear

    FLEXX

    African performance sportswear and team jerseys, built for early mornings, heavy sessions and game day. Born in Africa, built for the world.

    Daily · 9 am to 10 pm@flexx_now

  • Jewelry

    Melah Luxe

    Stainless steel jewelry and watches in gold and silver tones, from a female-owned Kigali brand. A little gold, a little silver, a lot of Melah.

    Daily · 9 am to 8 pm@melah.luxe

    Good to know

    Finding your way

    Every shop here is in the Zaria Court retail park on KG 17 Ave, Remera, a short walk from the hotel. The team at reception can point you in the right direction.

    Opening hours

    All twelve shops open every day except Wok Spot, which closes on Mondays. Hours can change on public holidays and event days, so check with the shop if you are cutting it close.

    Events and bookings

    Event details come from the Zaria Court team and can change, so check the event link before you set off. Courts and pitches are booked on Ticqet.
