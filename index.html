    Good to know

    Finding your way

    Every shop is in the Zaria Court retail park on KG 17 Ave, Remera, a short walk from the hotel. The team at reception can point you in the right direction.

    Get directions (opens in a new tab)

    Opening hours

    All twelve shops open every day except Wok Spot, which closes on Mondays. Hours can change on public holidays and event days, so check with the shop if you are cutting it close.

    Events and bookings

    Event details come from the Zaria Court team and can change, so check the event link before you set off. Pitches and the court are booked on Ticqet.
